About Gadasru Mahadev Lake Trek

Gadasru Mahadev Lake is one of the most beautiful and spiritually significant high-altitude lakes in the Chamba region of Himachal Pradesh. Surrounded by lush green meadows and towering Himalayan peaks, the lake offers a peaceful escape into nature.

The trek passes through dense forests, traditional mountain trails and scenic landscapes before reaching the crystal-clear waters of Gadasru Lake. The lake holds religious importance among local devotees and attracts trekkers looking for both adventure and natural beauty.
