About Kugti Pass Trek

Kugti Pass Trek is one of the most challenging and rewarding trekking routes in Himachal Pradesh. Located in the Pir Panjal Range, the trek offers dramatic mountain landscapes, glaciers, alpine meadows and remote Himalayan wilderness.

Starting from the historic Kugti Village near Bharmour, the trail takes trekkers through ancient shepherd routes and breathtaking valleys before reaching the magnificent Kugti Pass at over 5,000 metres. This trek is ideal for experienced trekkers seeking a true Himalayan expedition.
