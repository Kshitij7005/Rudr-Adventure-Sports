About Manimahesh Lake Trek

Manimahesh Lake Trek is one of the most famous and sacred trekking routes in Himachal Pradesh. Located at the base of the majestic Manimahesh Kailash Peak, the lake holds immense religious significance and attracts thousands of pilgrims and trekkers every year.

The trek begins from Hadsar Village and passes through beautiful mountain trails, waterfalls, alpine meadows and breathtaking Himalayan scenery. The crystal-clear waters of Manimahesh Lake reflecting the towering Kailash Peak create an unforgettable experience for visitors.
