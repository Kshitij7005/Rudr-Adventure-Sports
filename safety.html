Safety Guidelines

At Rudr Adventure Sports, safety is our top priority. All activities are conducted under strict supervision of trained professionals.

General Safety Rules

✔ Always listen to your instructor
✔ Wear safety gear provided by us
✔ Do not panic during activities
✔ Follow all instructions carefully

River Rafting Safety

Life jackets and helmets are mandatory. Only trained guides handle rafting operations. Swimming ability is helpful but not required.

Paragliding Safety

Tandem flights are operated by certified pilots. All equipment is regularly checked and maintained.

Trekking Safety

Always stay in group and follow the guide. Carry proper shoes and water bottles.
